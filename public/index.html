Audio Visualizer

Play music anywhere on your computer and watch it come to life!

  1. Press Start and choose Entire Screen in the share dialog.
  2. Tick Share system audio.
  3. Play some music anywhere on your computer!

Controls

  • Move your pointer: pull particles toward it.
  • Click your pointer: create a shockwave.
  • Panel (top right): tweak sensitivity, looks, and particles.
  • ? (bottom left): reopen these instructions.

Works best in desktop Chrome or Edge. Nothing is recorded or saved. If sharing fails with a "Your audio device blocked sharing" error, try a different output device, share a browser tab instead of Entire Screen, or use the demo sound below. This is due to driver and browser limitations.
